# Study overview (record template)

When a new study is to be judged with plr-cmm-agent, put this one sheet in the study's plan folder ("1．計画" in the example config.yaml). Name the file "000_overview.md" (docx or txt is also fine). Existing records can stay as they are. Write only facts that are in the records. Do not write personal names, contact details, or the responses or states of individual people. Names of organizations and roles may be written. Write numbers (people, items, percentages, dates) as the records give them.

The nine headings are the same as the topics of the notes written by the reading step. Write "none" under a heading that does not apply.

The level is the number that stands for the capabilities the organization acquired in its earlier studies. In judging, capabilities at or below this level are premises and are not judged from the records; only the capabilities above the level are judged. Never write another organization's level on an organization's records. An organization with no history writes "none", and all 16 capabilities are judged from the records.

Level: (the highest level whose capabilities the organization all uses in routine operation: L1 to L5, or none. PLR is L3. Capabilities at or below this level are premises and are not judged from the records)

Name: (a short name for the "Study" column of the judgment table, e.g. Account opening for older participants)

Period: (YYYY-MM to YYYY-MM)

## Purpose and data

(The purpose of the study. The data items handled and their counts. The conditions for participants and their number)

## Stakeholders and roles

(The organizations and roles: data subjects, primary users, secondary users, intermediaries, the review body. Who did what)

## Consent method

(The form of consent: broad consent or per-request consent. Whether withdrawal was provided for, and whether it happened. How the explanation was given)

## Review, governance and security

(The review body and its result. Counts and reasons of approvals, returns and rejections. Data storage and safeguards)

## Transactions, compensation and return of outcomes

(Usage agreements, compensation or rewards, and whether outcomes or results were returned to data subjects or primary users)

## Participation support and proxy

(Support for people who cannot take part by themselves, proxy participation, identity verification)

## Systems, migration and interoperability

(The systems used, connections to other platforms or IDs, migrations or changes)

## Results and operation

(Counts of accounts and consents, what was achieved, what remains. Whether it entered routine operation)

## Other

(Facts that fit none of the headings above)
